Signed workspace rules, dual mode. A rules document, and the workspace's new signed "no rules for this computer" answer (`{ type: "scopebond:managed-rules", managed: false, … }` with status 200), may carry an Ed25519 `signature` and `key_id` over the RFC 8785 canonical JSON of `{ v: 1, workspace, computer, revision, rules_digest, local_changes, managed, issued_at }`, built from this installation's own workspace and computer ids. Trusted keys are pinned in the release (`src/policy-keys.ts`, none yet; `SCOPEBOND_POLICY_KEYS_JSON` adds keys; an entry with an inverted window or an unreadable key is left out and `doctor` says why). In `dual` mode (the default) a signed answer must verify, with a known key inside its validity window, for this computer and workspace, not older than the one in force and not more than an hour ahead of this computer's clock, under a key whose window holds both the answer's `issued_at` and the present moment; the version in force served again is checked too; an unsigned answer is accepted and recorded as `unsigned_policy`. In `enforce` mode (`SCOPEBOND_POLICY_SIGNATURE_MODE=enforce` or `policy_signature_mode` in `cloud.json`) an unsigned answer, a 204 or a 401 is refused and the policy in force stays. `status --json` reports `policy_signature: { mode, last, reason }`, `status` and `doctor` show it, and each rules check sends it (`x-scopebond-policy-signature`).
