The Scopebond Agent is installed.

It starts with your sign-in to this Mac and, the first time, opens your workspace's sign-in page in your browser. To connect it to a workspace yourself, run this in Terminal:

    scopebond-agent setup <workspace-url>

"scopebond-agent status" shows where it stands.

To remove it, run this in Terminal (add --purge to delete your Scopebond folder too):

    sudo "/Library/Application Support/Scopebond/uninstall.sh"

Help: https://scopebond.com/documentation
